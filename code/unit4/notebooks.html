1 Angle estimation with the Bayes’ filter

The cleanest illustration of the filter itself. The angle of a spinning wheel is to be estimated from a camera that reads the hue of coloured markings on the wheel, but sees only a limited arc of it and, because brightness varies, sometimes reports the wrong hue – though usually one close to the truth. The angle is quantized into \(N\) values, so the belief is just a vector of \(N\) probabilities, and the prediction and correction steps of the Bayes’ filter can be watched updating it directly. This is the discrete counterpart of the Kalman filter example that opens Unit 6, and of the Viterbi notebook in Unit 1, which asks for the most likely sequence of angles rather than the belief at each step.

Download A_4_angleestimationbayes.ipynb

2 Position estimation in a grid world

An agent moves through the 77-cell grid world of Unit 3, plus 40 obstacle cells in which it is invisible, taking a step to an adjacent cell or staying put with equal probability among the moves the walls permit. When the agent is visible its position is measured; when it is not, no measurement arrives at all. The belief over its position must therefore be propagated through stretches of pure prediction and sharpened again when the agent reappears – a good demonstration that a missing measurement is itself informative, since it rules out every visible cell.

Download A_4_K_gridworldBayes.ipynb

3 Remote temperature sensing: when should a sensor transmit?

A battery-powered sensor measures a temperature evolving as \(T_{k+1}=T_k+U_k+w_k\) and transmits to a remote station over a wireless link, where transmissions are the dominant drain on the battery. The question is how to keep the remote estimate accurate while transmitting as little as possible, and the notebook compares two answers: transmit periodically, or transmit only when the measurement has drifted past a threshold. The threshold rule is the more interesting one, because silence then carries information – the station learns that the temperature is still within the band – which is exactly the kind of reasoning the Bayes’ filter formalizes.
